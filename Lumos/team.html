Leadership & Team

Gururaj Rao

FOUNDER-DIRECTOR, LUMOS.IN

Gururaj Rao has been leading Lumos for over two decades. His passion for purpose and impact, both in professional and social-impact spaces has enabled him to innovate and build a solution-centric model for all clients, across industries, globally. An entrepreneur, brand consultant, creative strategist, and mentor, with a Bachelors in Engineering from Bangalore University, Karnataka, India. A senior industry thought leader, he has been delivering transformational solutions for companies and individuals, globally.

 

Professional experience which spans close to 30 years in Creative Media and IT industry, ranging from Positioning & Branding, Digital Marketing, Online Presence, Designing & Architecting solutions, for a wide range of market segments including Healthcare, Real Estate & Property development, Entertainment, IT & ITES, Banking, NGOs, Not-for-profit organizations and spiritual organizations.

 

Raj is known for his perseverance, a grit to deliver solutions to build successful and scalable business models for clients, combined with a new-age design-thinking, innovative leaning to enhance equity, success, presence for brands and leaders.
